How billing, replacements and refunds work on CrackHub.

1. How billing works

Access is covered by your monthly subscription — there is no per-account price list and generations are never billed individually. One active subscription unlocks every service on the store.

  • Renew any time from the store page: pay the monthly amount, upload your receipt, the admin approves it.
  • Your subscription badge always shows the status and how many days are left.
  • Pool counters and your usage history are visible any time from the store page.
  • Older key balances are kept — anything that was charged before can still be refunded to them.

2. Reporting a dead account

Accounts can die minutes after delivery (password changes, sessions invalidated, region locks). Report it right away:

  • Open Support and pick the generation from the dropdown (or paste the account).
  • Describe exactly what happens: wrong password, verification prompt, "restricted", etc.
  • The same account cannot be reported twice while a ticket is still open.
  • The admin answers inside the ticket thread — you can reply with more details any time.
Turnaround: tickets are normally handled within a few hours. You will get either a fresh replacement account or, when something was already charged, a refund of that exact amount.

3. Refund & replacement policy

  • Account never worked — full refund or a free replacement, your choice.
  • Worked, then died within 24 hours — replacement or refund.
  • Worked for days — replacement at the admin's discretion, no refund.
  • Heavy use, changed credentials or cancelled subscription — no refund.
Refunds are credited to your key balance, never paid out in cash. Refunded generations stay in your activity list and are marked with the ticket number.

4. Fair-use rules

  • Do not resell keys, accounts or the bulk credential tools without permission.
  • Do not generate repeatedly just to "check" a pool — it drains accounts other members could use.
  • Do not share a key between people. Keys are per-user and traceable.
  • Abusing the ticket system (false reports, duplicate tickets) can disable a key without refund.
  • Only use the accounts for personal access to the services your subscription covers.
Disabling: a key can be paused at any time for abuse or chargebacks. Outstanding balance is preserved and usable again once the key is re-enabled.

5. Keeping your key safe

  • Your key works like a password — never post it in public chats or screenshots.
  • Think it leaked? Ask the admin to rotate it — a new key is issued instantly and yours stops working.
  • Sessions last 30 days; use Log out on shared devices.

Questions about billing? Open a ticket — your usage history (service, account, exact time) is attached automatically so nobody has to guess.
